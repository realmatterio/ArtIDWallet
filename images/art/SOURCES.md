# Demo artwork sources

Public-domain paintings, used as demo content for the resale prototype.
Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, centre-cropped square and resized to 560px.

| File | Work | Artist | Died | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `scream.jpg` | The Scream, 1893 | Edvard Munch | 1944 | Wikimedia Commons |
| `great-wave.jpg` | The Great Wave off Kanagawa, c.1831 | Katsushika Hokusai | 1849 | Wikimedia Commons |
| `starry-night.jpg` | The Starry Night, 1889 | Vincent van Gogh | 1890 | Wikimedia Commons |
| `sunflowers.jpg` | Sunflowers, 1888 | Vincent van Gogh | 1890 | Wikimedia Commons |

All four artists died well over 70 years ago, so the paintings are in the public
domain worldwide. Faithful photographic reproductions of public-domain
two-dimensional art do not attract a new copyright (US: *Bridgeman v. Corel*;
EU: Directive 2019/790 Art. 14).

Note this covers the *paintings*. Andy Warhol's screenprints after Munch remain
in copyright and are licensed by the Andy Warhol Foundation - none are used here.
